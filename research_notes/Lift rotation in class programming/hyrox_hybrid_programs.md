# HYROX-style and hybrid group-class programs: weekly templates, lift rotation and cycles

Scope note: researched Nov 2026 with about 20 search/fetch calls. Most commercial programs (HYROX365, PRVN, ROXFIT, HWPO, Hybrid Performance Method, Nick Bare app plans) keep their day-by-day content behind a paywall or affiliate login. Their public pages describe formats and philosophy, not lift-by-lift templates. Pages I found confirm structure at the level of formats, tracks and cycle length. Lift-selection details come mostly from free third-party plans and blogs, which are lower-quality sources. Each bullet is marked with what it rests on.

## Q1. Weekly template: how many strength days, which lifts/patterns, and do they rotate week to week or block to block?

### Takeaway
No source I found describes a fixed lift per weekday repeated week after week for HYROX group classes. Official and big-vendor programs organize the week by class format or "focus" (strength/power, engine, race-specific, foundational), and the program rotates them. The free individual plans that do name days use pattern labels ("squat focus" / "hinge focus") or generic "gym strength" days and change emphasis phase to phase. Two strength-leaning sessions a week is the most common recommendation.

### Cited Findings
**Official HYROX365 / HYROX Training Club**
- HYROX365 programming "offer[s] four different focal points in training". The Performance Hub provides "a library of workouts, different training collections, progressive tracks" and is pitched as "a complete year-round programming solution". No weekly template is published publicly. — [HYROX365 Training Club page](https://www.hyrox365.com/affiliation/hyrox-training-club)
- The four HYROX365 class formats are Foundational, Engine, Power and Complete. GoodLife (Canada) runs these four at 35+ gyms. Gold's Gym Tampa lists five, adding a separate Strength class. — [GoodLife HYROX Training Club](https://www.goodlifefitness.com/hyrox-training-club.html); [Gold's Gym Tampa](https://goldsgymgasworx.com/hyrox-training-club/) (as summarized in search results; the pages themselves were not fetched)
- A HYROX Training Club operator (Czech Republic) describes the formats:
  - **Foundational:** "Circuit training with a purpose", mixing strength, endurance, technique and mobility. Scales to beginners.
  - **Engine:** aerobic power, threshold, erg efficiency. For "strong people who lack endurance".
  - **Power:** "maximal and repeated strength, strength endurance and power… moving load over time". For runners who "run out of strength on the sled, lunges and farmers carry".
  - **Complete:** "HYROX in training form", with runs, stations and transitions.
  - On scheduling: "You don't pick the focus. The programme rotates it for you," and members cover all four over the weeks. — [HYROX Training Club CZ](https://hyroxtrainingclub.cz/en/)
- The same operator's separate 12-week race-prep course uses "4 sessions a week", made up of "2 runs + 2 strength / HYROX sessions". — [HYROX Training Club CZ](https://hyroxtrainingclub.cz/en/)
- Wodify's marketplace listing says HYROX365 delivers four distinct class formats so different archetypes can train effectively, and calls "Complete" the most race-like. The page returned 403 to direct fetch, so this rests on the search snippet only. — [Wodify HYROX365 listing](https://www.wodify.com/workout-marketplace/hyrox365)

**PRVN HYROX (James Kelly)**
- The PRVN HYROX Affiliate 7-day track is "progressive, race-inspired hybrid training 7 days a week". Strength is aimed at supporting sled push/pull, lunges, carries and wall balls. The public page names no specific lifts or rotation. — [PRVN HYROX Affiliate 7-Day on BTWB](https://programs.btwb.com/prvn/hyrox_affiliate_7)
- PRVN's 3-day track assumes athletes get strength elsewhere. In the 7-day track, strength is "primarily programmed on Mondays and Wednesdays", outside the 3-day structure. This rests on the search-result summary of PRVN's product page; a direct fetch hit HTTP 429. — [PRVN HYROX Affiliate Google Doc subscription](https://prvnfitness.com/products/prvn-hyrox-affiliate-google-doc-subscription)
- James Kelly writes the PRVN HYROX program, which launched in 2025. — [BarBend](https://barbend.com/news/james-kelly-to-coach-new-prvn-hyrox-program/)

**Third-party affiliate programming (The Hybrid Engine)**
- The Affiliate Track has five 60-minute class sessions, "1 big Hyrox specific weekend class day" and "1 extended active recovery/cardio conditioning day". Each class has a minute-by-minute "Class Flow" for warm-up → strength → GPP, plus coach's notes on setup, intent and managing levels. No lift rotation is described publicly. — [The Hybrid Engine](https://www.thehybridengine.com/)

**PushPress case study (multi-track gym)**
- One gym runs five HYROX formats on a strength-to-aerobic continuum: Strong (sets and reps, often barbell), Total (MB/KB/DB/sandbag/sled, longer moderate-load work, "muscular stamina"), Race (race-pace tempo with compromised running), Interval (threshold intervals) and Capacity (long moderate base work). — [PushPress blog: 5-track HYROX class rotation](https://www.pushpress.com/blog/5-track-hyrox-class-rotation-doubled-race-signups)

**Free individual plans (lower-quality sources)**
- **Hybrid Pro Coach:** Mon gym strength, Tue run, Wed gym strength, Thu run, Fri HYROX/compromised, Sat long run, Sun recovery. Lifts are listed by exercise, not by day: back squat 4x6–8, deadlift 3–5x5, RDL 3x10–12, OHP 3–4x8–10, bent-over row 3–4x8–10, weighted lunges 3x12–16 steps. No rotation or blocks are described. — [Hybrid Pro Coach gym workout plan](https://hybridprocoach.com/blogs/hyrox/hyrox-gym-workout-plan)
- **MyTrainerApp 12-week plan:** Tue "Strength, squat focus" and Thu "Strength, hinge focus" in weeks 1–4. From week 5 the same days become "Strength plus sled push 4x30 m" and "Strength plus sandbag lunges 4x30 m", then "strength plus heavy sled" in weeks 9–11. Days are defined by pattern; no lifts are named. — [MyTrainerApp](https://www.mytrainerapp.io/en/blog/hyrox-training-plan)
- **RMR Training:** strength focus changes by phase, from strength base to strength power to strength endurance, across 12–16 weeks. — [RMR Training](https://www.rmr.training/blog/a-step-by-step-guide-on-how-to-create-your-own-hyrox-training-program) (search snippet)
- **Boostcamp "GZCL x HYROX":** Day 1 upper strength + HYROX finisher, Day 2 lower strength + run intervals, Day 3 full-body strength + HYROX circuit, Day 4 steady run + mobility/light strength. — [Boostcamp](https://www.boostcamp.app/users/9g409I-gzcl-x-hyrox) (search snippet)
- **StrengthLog beginner hybrid:** strength Mon and Thu, cardio Tue, intervals/event prep Fri or Sat. — [StrengthLog 12-week beginner hybrid](https://www.strengthlog.com/12-week-beginner-hybrid-athlete-program/) (search snippet)
- **Nick Bare's free 16-week hybrid plan:** Mon–Sat, about five lifts and four runs a week. "Each lift is trained 1x weekly", which is a classic fixed body-part/lift split. The paid "Hybrid Athlete 1.0" app opens with "Block 1 – Push Strength + Run"; later blocks were not visible. — [Lift Vault](https://liftvault.com/programs/running/nick-bare-16-week-hybrid-training-program-spreadsheet/); [Playbook: Hybrid Athlete 1.0](https://my.playbookapp.io/nick-bare/programs/hybrid-athlete-10/953)
- Free guides recommend anywhere from 1 to 3 strength sessions a week, with 2 often called the sweet spot. Some split three days into legs/push/pull. — [repz.app strength guide](https://www.repz.app/blog/hyrox-strength-training); [roxzone.training](https://roxzone.training/blog/hyrox-training-methodology)

### Inferences
- The dominant HYROX group-class model is **rotating focus/format days**, not "Monday = back squat forever". Strength lives mainly in one format (Power/Strong), with strength-endurance in a second (Foundational/Total). In a Mon–Fri class, that works out to 2 strength-leaning days a week.
- Where lifts are named, they cover a small pool of patterns: squat (back/front), hinge (deadlift/RDL), lunge, horizontal pull (row), overhead push, carries. A rotating pattern pool (squat-day / hinge-day, with the exact lift changing by block) matches how the better free plans are written.
- Nick Bare's "each lift 1x weekly" split is the clearest "fixed lift per day" example found. It is an individual bodybuilding-influenced plan, not a group class.

### Gaps
- ROXFIT (Hunter McIntyre), HWPO/Fraser hybrid, Hybrid Performance Method, Ben Thurley, Rich Ryan and Alexander Rončević: none of my searches surfaced public weekly templates or lift-rotation descriptions. These programs are app- or paywall-gated, and I could not verify anything about them.
- I could not see actual HYROX365 or PRVN day content, so I don't know whether their Power/strength days repeat the same lift weekly within a block.
- No Reddit r/hyrox threads surfaced in search.

## Q2. Do they use A/B weeks, movement-pattern days rather than specific lifts, or undulating schemes?

### Takeaway
The public material shows a format rotation (HYROX365, PushPress gym) and pattern-labelled days (squat-focus / hinge-focus). I found no explicit A/B-week or daily-undulating scheme documented for HYROX classes. Phase-to-phase changes in strength quality (base → power → strength-endurance) are the most common progression.

### Cited Findings
- HYROX365 rotates the four focal formats for members: "The programme rotates it for you." — [HYROX Training Club CZ](https://hyroxtrainingclub.cz/en/)
- PushPress case-study gym: unique weekend workouts in five formats are recycled onto weekdays. A Saturday 7:15 AM workout returns as the Monday workout nine days later, giving a 9-day recycle loop. Members can double up, for example Strong followed by Total. — [PushPress blog](https://www.pushpress.com/blog/5-track-hyrox-class-rotation-doubled-race-signups)
- Days are labelled by pattern ("squat focus", "hinge focus"), not by lift. — [MyTrainerApp](https://www.mytrainerapp.io/en/blog/hyrox-training-plan)
- Strength quality changes by phase: base → power → endurance. — [RMR Training](https://www.rmr.training/blog/a-step-by-step-guide-on-how-to-create-your-own-hyrox-training-program) (snippet)
- One plan rotates stations so all eight appear every two weeks, a de facto A/B fortnight for stations, and raises sled load 5–10% a week. — [FitVizPro](https://www.fitvizpro.com/hyrox-training-plan) (search snippet)
- Progression on fixed lifts: add 2.5 kg every 1–2 weeks, then reps, then sets (double progression). The plan warns of plateaus within 4–6 weeks without overload. — [Hybrid Pro Coach](https://hybridprocoach.com/blogs/hyrox/hyrox-gym-workout-plan)
- EliteFTS argues for a conjugate-style organization for HYROX ("organization beats volume"). Conjugate rotates max-effort exercise variations on a short cycle. — [EliteFTS: Conjugate for HYROX](https://elitefts.com/blogs/training/conjugate-for-hyrox-why-organization-beats-volume) (title/snippet only; not fetched)

### Inferences
- A fortnightly A/B station rotation (all 8 stations every 2 weeks) combined with two pattern days per week (lower-squat/lunge vs hinge/pull) is a well-supported design.
- Keeping the same main lift for a 3–6 week block and then swapping variation is consistent with the double-progression and plateau guidance above. Rotating daily would undercut measurable progress for a general-population group.

### Gaps
- No primary source shows HYROX365 or PRVN using A/B weeks or DUP specifically.
- I did not fetch the EliteFTS conjugate article, so I don't know its specifics.

## Q3. How are stations and compromised running distributed across the week/cycle? How is novelty handled?

### Takeaway
Programs isolate race-specific "compromised running" and multi-station work into one format or day per week (Complete / Race / a weekend HYROX day). Stations rotate so all eight are covered over one to two weeks. Variety comes from format (solo, partner, intervals, mixed-modal) rather than from new movements, since the eight stations are fixed.

### Cited Findings
- The PRVN 7-day track mixes solo efforts, partner formats, race simulations and mixed-modal workouts, but doesn't tie these to specific days. Its sample day used a run + farmer carry + calorie erg + wall ball couplet in three tiers. — [PRVN on BTWB](https://programs.btwb.com/prvn/hyrox_affiliate_7)
- PushPress "Race" format: "extended running intervals mixed with intervals of the movement of the day". It is explicitly not a full simulation. — [PushPress blog](https://www.pushpress.com/blog/5-track-hyrox-class-rotation-doubled-race-signups)
- PushPress says a dedicated HYROX track run 2–3 times a week can coexist with a regular schedule. Members should train all 8 stations plus running across a weekly or biweekly rotation. — [PushPress (search summary)](https://www.pushpress.com/blog/what-is-hyrox-guide-gym-owners)
- A 10-week "Road to Navy Pier" program used weekly Sunday sessions on compromised running with one HYROX station per week. Earlier versions gave each station one week with a station time trial. — [PushPress blog](https://www.pushpress.com/blog/5-track-hyrox-class-rotation-doubled-race-signups)
- The Hybrid Engine concentrates the HYROX-specific work in "1 big Hyrox specific weekend class day". — [The Hybrid Engine](https://www.thehybridengine.com/)
- HYROX Training Club CZ: "Complete" covers running, stations, transitions and race rhythm. "Power" covers sled, lunges and carries. — [HYROX Training Club CZ](https://hyroxtrainingclub.cz/en/)
- Phased plans add station work onto strength days in the build phase (sled push, sandbag lunges) and save half simulations (4 stations + 4 km) and full simulations for weeks 9–11. — [MyTrainerApp](https://www.mytrainerapp.io/en/blog/hyrox-training-plan)
- Operator cautions: one owner who ran HYROX for a year went back to normal endurance programming, and the class grew again. Another says "Don't treat it as CrossFit light." — [Morning Chalk Up](https://morningchalkup.com/2025/10/17/hyrox-is-booming-should-crossfit-gyms-add-hyrox-affiliation/) (as summarized by search tool; attribution of individual quotes not verified on-page)
- The Progrm (John Singleton) says HYROX classes should complement CrossFit, not replace it. Programming is built to be modified on existing gear "to provide the right stimulus". Running needs planned routes and station zones. — [BarBend: CrossFit meets HYROX pt 3](https://barbend.com/news/crossfit-meets-hyrox-part-3/)

### Inferences
- For a "quiet HYROX" Mon–Fri class, the simplest mapping is: 2 strength-pattern days with station-flavoured finishers (lunges, carries, wall balls), 1–2 engine/interval days with run plus erg, and 1 "complete"/compromised-running day, perhaps Friday. Rotate the featured stations so each appears at least every 2 weeks.
- Novelty can come from format (partner, EMOM, ladder, AMRAP) layered over a stable pattern and station pool.

### Gaps
- No source gives actual weekly station counts or run volume per class for HYROX365 or PRVN.

## Q4. How do programs substitute for sled push/pull and SkiErg?

### Takeaway
Substitution is commonplace in commercial gyms. Common sled-push stand-ins are heavy squats/Zercher walks, plate or treadmill pushes and car pushes. Sled-pull stand-ins are rows, cable/rope hand-over-hand pulls, sandbag drags and backward walking. SkiErg stand-ins are rower/bike intervals, upper-back pulling and banded or med-ball patterns. Sources consistently say transfer is only partial.

### Cited Findings
- **Gymshark:** without a sled, use "treadmill pushes, cable machine pulls, or sandbag drags", which build "the same drive, control, and grip endurance". — [Gymshark solo HYROX](https://www.gymshark.com/blog/article/solo-hyrox)
- **MyTrainerApp, "Training without a sled":**
  - Push: car pushes, heavy walking Zercher squats, slow-tempo box squats, weighted plate pushes.
  - Pull: weighted ring rows, seated cable rows, heavy band face pulls, low-pulley pulls, max-resistance rowing efforts.
  - Carries: DB/KB farmer and suitcase carries.
  — [MyTrainerApp](https://www.mytrainerapp.io/en/blog/hyrox-training-plan)
- **Hybrid Pro Coach:** "Deadlifts replace sled pulls for posterior chain power", "Back squats replace sled pushes for leg drive strength", OHP and thrusters replace wall balls, and bent-over rows build pulling. The SkiErg "helps but is not essential". — [Hybrid Pro Coach](https://hybridprocoach.com/blogs/hyrox/hyrox-gym-workout-plan)
- **repz.app sled-pull alternatives:** alternating cable or rope hand-over-hand pulls (the "most complete" single option), horizontal rows (3x15), loaded carries, RDL/good mornings, backward walking, battle ropes (6 x 20s on/40s off → 30/30) and towel pull-ups. Caveats: no exact substitute exists, no validated kg conversions, and specific sled practice is still needed for technique, friction and rope handling. — [repz.app](https://www.repz.app/blog/sled-pull-alternatives)
- **TRX:** straps attached to a sled, walking back, then pulling it in. This assumes a sled is available. — [HYROX Hub Substack](https://hyroxhub.substack.com/p/how-to-crush-the-sled-pull) (snippet)
- **SkiErg:** bodyweight plans replace it with high knees or shadow-boxing arm work for 4–5 min. — [Chairon](https://chairon.app/blogs/hyrox-bodyweight-workout) (snippet)
- **Community suggestions:** treadmill or assault bike intervals for engine work, DB thrusters or med-ball slams for wall balls, banded pulls for the SkiErg. — [Skool Hybrid Racer thread](https://www.skool.com/hybridracer/hyrox-prep-without-the-equipment?p=84d335b1) (snippet; community, low authority)
- **Erg circuits:** mixed row/bike/ski circuits (3 min each + 1 min rest, 4–6 rounds) target the same heart-rate zones. The rower and bike are named as stand-ins for aerobic demand. The SkiErg station heavily taxes large upper-body muscles that are "less conditioned" for high-volume repetitive pulling. — [BoxRox](https://www.boxrox.com/?p=238382); [PureGym SkiErg](https://www.puregym.com/blog/hyrox-skierg/) (snippets)
- **Men's Health:** publishes a HYROX simulation "you can do in any gym" using substitutions. — [Men's Health SA](https://mh.co.za/?p=248454) (snippet)
- **PRVN:** its sample warm-up offers row or ski per interval, so erg choice is interchangeable at the warm-up level. — [PRVN on BTWB](https://programs.btwb.com/prvn/hyrox_affiliate_7)

### Inferences
- For a gym with no sled and no SkiErg:
  - Sled push: heavy plate or prowler-style pushes on turf or floor if available; otherwise heavy Zercher/front-rack walking lunges, tempo box squats and wall-sit-to-drive drills.
  - Sled pull: hand-over-hand rope pulls on a cable, or a sandbag drag with rope; heavy rows; backward walks with a band or sandbag.
  - SkiErg: the rower at a ski-like cadence; standing banded straight-arm pulldowns or med-ball slams in intervals.
- Present these substitutions as building the pattern and engine, not race-day skill.

### Gaps
- I found no study or official HYROX guidance validating any substitute. HYROX365's official substitution list, if one exists, is not public.

## Q5. Cycle structure: test/retest, simulation weeks, taper, length

### Takeaway
Public cycle lengths cluster at 8 weeks (PRVN), 10 weeks (an affiliate race program), 12 weeks (HYROX Training Club race prep and most free plans) and 8–12 weeks (F45). Typical arcs run base → build → specific → taper. Simulations (half, then full) are concentrated in the final 2–3 weeks. Test/retest exists mainly as run time trials and benchmark workouts.

### Cited Findings
- PRVN: "Structured 8-week cycles to keep you improving nonstop", "Year-Round Training Cycles", and a "12-WK HYROX PROGRAM" at PRVN HQ. Reviews mention a "10k trial at start of 8 week cycle" and a 5 km run retest; these are anecdotal. — [PRVN HYROX program page](https://prvnfitness.com/pages/prvn-hyrox-program)
- PRVN affiliate programming uses "block-based progression in line with the HYROX season" with "seasonally themed training blocks". — [PRVN on BTWB](https://programs.btwb.com/prvn/hyrox_affiliate_7)
- HYROX Training Clubs may run "PFT and Benchmark Workouts" and small events, labelled as not official HYROX events. — [HYROX365 Training Club page](https://www.hyrox365.com/affiliation/hyrox-training-club)
- MyTrainerApp 12-week plan:
  - Weeks 1–4: base. Weeks 5–8: build. Weeks 9–11: specific, with a half simulation (4 stations + 4 km) on Thursday and a full simulation at race weights on Saturday. Week 12: taper.
  - Taper keeps light strength with "no sets to failure" and strides.
  - No testing protocol.
  — [MyTrainerApp](https://www.mytrainerapp.io/en/blog/hyrox-training-plan)
- Affiliate race program: 10 weeks, with station time trials in earlier versions. — [PushPress blog](https://www.pushpress.com/blog/5-track-hyrox-class-rotation-doubled-race-signups)
- F45 HYROX Prep Program: an 8–12 week track for already-active "hybrid athletes". F45 classifies its regular classes as Cardio, Resistance, Hybrid and Recovery. — [F45 HYROX 101](https://f45training.com/article/hyrox-training-101/); [F45 HYROX Prep (mirror)](https://2d93f66b-714b6bd10aefc79aa686acff6.pages.mailchi.mp.f45training.com/?p=143835) (snippets)
- A third-party blog says F45's short-burst format doesn't replicate sustained Zone 4–5 HYROX effort, so dedicated running and transition practice should be added. — [Chairon (Dec 2025)](https://chairon.app/blogs/f45-hyrox-training) (secondary)
- Fitness First, an official HYROX training partner, runs a "HYROX Sim" class built on run plus stations (SkiErg, sled, row, wall balls). — [Fitness First HYROX Sim](https://fitnessfirst.co.uk/fitness-classes/performance/hyrox-sim) (snippet)

### Inferences
- An 8–12 week class cycle with a benchmark in week 1 and a retest at the end fits the market norm, for example a 1 km or 5 km run plus a short station benchmark. So does a "mini-sim" Friday in the last 2 weeks. For a non-race general-population class, the taper can be a simple deload week rather than a race taper.

### Gaps
- No public detail on HYROX365's block lengths or official test protocols.
- Orangetheory, Barry's and Gymshark do not publish HYROX class cycle structures that I found.

## Q6. What do they say about general population / masters members?

### Takeaway
Official messaging is "for Every Body". Concrete public guidance is limited to tiered scaling (Pro / Open / Foundation) and a beginner-friendly Foundational format. I found no masters-specific programming guidance.

### Cited Findings
- HYROX365 Training Club pitches "accessible Performance Training with a purpose for Every Body". It says "Any kind of gym is suitable to become a HYROX TRAINING CLUB" and that the program "optimizes the use of equipment and floor space to allow for maximum capacity classes". — [HYROX365 Training Club](https://www.hyrox365.com/affiliation/hyrox-training-club)
- Foundational is "ideal for beginners", and "You don't need experience or fitness. You need to show up." — [HYROX Training Club CZ](https://hyroxtrainingclub.cz/en/)
- PRVN three-tier scaling:
  - Pro: 2x32/24 kg KB, 9/6 kg wall ball.
  - Open: 2x24/16 kg, 6/4 kg.
  - Foundation: 300 m runs instead of 400 m, 15/10 cal instead of 20/15, 2x16/12 kg, lower wall-ball target.
  — [PRVN on BTWB](https://programs.btwb.com/prvn/hyrox_affiliate_7)
- PRVN offers "Leveled training options… for athletes of all fitness levels". — [PRVN program page](https://prvnfitness.com/pages/prvn-hyrox-program)
- The Hybrid Engine coach's notes cover "how to manage athletes at different levels within one class". — [The Hybrid Engine](https://www.thehybridengine.com/)
- The Progrm lists HYROX class uses that include an on-ramp for new members, active recovery or rehab, and preventing burnout in long-term members. — [BarBend](https://barbend.com/news/crossfit-meets-hyrox-part-3/)
- An AOL/Women's Health piece examines whether HYROX's "for every body" claim delivers. — [AOL](https://www.aol.com/hyrox-promises-race-every-body-185000096.html) (not fetched)

### Inferences
- For a 9am general-population or masters group, the pattern the market uses is:
  - Scale distance first (300 m vs 400 m runs), then load (one KB/DB tier down), then reps or calories.
  - Keep a strength day that is controlled sets and reps rather than to failure, as in the taper guidance above.
- Running can be swapped for the rower or bike for members with impact limits. BoxRox's "can't run" guidance supports this.

### Gaps
- I found no masters-specific HYROX class guidance from HYROX365, PRVN, ROXFIT or HWPO. The age-group race divisions exist, but no programming notes for them surfaced.
